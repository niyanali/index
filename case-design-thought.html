Case Study

DESIGN + THOUGHT

From Zero to 10+ Episodes — Building a Complete Production Pipeline

The Challenge

When All Right Technologies approached me, they had an ambitious vision: a professional podcast series. But they lacked the production infrastructure. They had a concept, a microphone, and raw ambition — but no experience with video production, no equipment, and no established workflow.

My task wasn't just to produce episodes. It was to design an entire production pipeline from scratch that could sustain 10+ episodes over 6 months while maintaining broadcast quality and creative consistency. I had to solve problems I'd never encountered before.

The Solution

Multi-Camera Setup: I designed a 3-camera system using available equipment — one wide camera for establishing shots, one medium for dialogue, one close-up for reactions. This created dynamic, cinematic interviews instead of static talking-head footage.

Lighting Design: I created a repeatable lighting rig using standard 2k and 1k lights to achieve professional results. Every episode could be lit identically, creating visual consistency across the entire series.

Editing Workflow: I established a standard editing template, naming conventions, and folder structure that allowed episodes to be produced at scale without sacrificing quality.

Color Grade & Graphics: I designed custom motion graphics, lower thirds, and established a signature color grade that became the show's visual identity.

Key Learnings

Systems > Hustle: I learned that good systems scale. When I designed repeatable workflows, quality didn't drop even as speed increased.

Visual Language Matters: Creating a "look" for a podcast (which is audio-first) seems absurd. But that visual identity became the show's signature. It made the brand memorable.

Communication is Production: The biggest challenge wasn't technical — it was coordinating with 10+ guests, managing schedules, and keeping stakeholders aligned.
