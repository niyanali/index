Case Study

NIKE — UNTAMED

Global campaign, high stakes, one concise visual rhythm.

The Brief

Nike gave me one instruction: make every shot feel raw, athletic, and urgent. The shoot had 14 days, 3 continents, and one very small window to capture the energy of movement.

This case study is about how I turned that pressure into a coherent visual story using motion, contrast, and pacing.

How it Worked

Movement-first framing: I built each shot around the athlete, then let the frame come alive through intentional camera movement.

Editing tempo: The cut rhythm mirrored breath and impact — fast when the motion was explosive, slower when the athlete prepared.

Lighting choices: High contrast, practical highlights, and strong shadow separation gave the campaign its cinematic edge.
